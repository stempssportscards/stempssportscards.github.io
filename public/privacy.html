Privacy

Stemp's Sports Cards stores your cards, photos and prices only in your own Google Drive, in files this app creates. We don't run a server or database and don't collect, sell or share your data.

If you use the card scanner, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini service only to identify the card. Buyer links show only the cards you mark for sale, never your cost or profit. You can remove the app's access any time in your Google Account settings.
